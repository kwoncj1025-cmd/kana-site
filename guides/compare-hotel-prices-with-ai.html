Hand off hotel price comparison to an AI agent

November 28, 2026 · YOOANPAPA

Opening Booking and Agoda tab by tab is tedious. Here is how Kana compares hotel prices in about 30–40 seconds, with the real on-screen price, a link and the time checked for each row.

Comparing hotels means the same dates typed into three sites, a dozen tabs, and prices that change while you look. It's exactly the kind of chore an agent should take.

What can go wrong with AI hotel searches

  • Made-up or stale prices. A chatbot answering from memory can't know tonight's rate.
  • No way to check. A price without a link means doing the search again yourself.
  • Booking without asking. An agent should never pay for a room on its own.

With Kana

  1. Say something like “Compare hotels in Tokyo for two nights.” If you skip dates or guests, Kana picks sensible defaults and tells you which ones she used.
  2. She opens Booking.com and Agoda in her own browser, the way a person would, and reads the prices shown on screen.
  3. You get 4–5 hotels in one list. Each row has the price as the site shows it, which site it came from, the time she checked, and an Open link straight to that page.
  4. Ads are marked as ads. If a site shows a robot check, she stops and tells you instead of trying to get around it.
  5. Booking is your call: reserving or paying always stops at an approval card.

Real numbers

Measured on the November 28, 2026 release, in Korean and English: about 30–40 seconds per comparison, no approval cards needed (reading only), Booking and Agoda both opened, five Open links, and in our check the listed prices matched what the sites showed.

Good to know

  • Prices change. Use the Open link to confirm before you book.
  • Kana reads what the page shows; member-only or app-only deals may differ.
  • She does not scrape sites in bulk. She opens a couple of pages, only when you ask.
