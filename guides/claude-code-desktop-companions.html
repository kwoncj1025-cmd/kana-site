Claude Code desktop companions, sorted

November 27, 2026 · YOOANPAPA

A quick map of desktop companions for Claude Code users: pixel pets that show your session status, notch widgets, and a character that runs errands with approval.

If you live in Claude Code, a small companion on the desktop can make long sessions feel less lonely, and some can do real work. They fall into two groups.

Status companions

These watch your Claude Code sessions and show what's happening: thinking, running a tool, waiting for your permission, done. Many are open source. Examples people share include pixel pets that sit next to the terminal, pets that grow as you use Claude Code, and notch or menu-bar widgets that list running sessions. They're light, fun and free, and they help you notice when Claude is waiting for you.

Errand companions

Instead of watching your coding session, these take separate tasks ("sort my calendar", "draft a reply", "find that file") and do them in the background while you keep coding.

What to look for

  • Does it only show status, or can it act? Both are useful; they solve different problems.
  • How does it ask permission? If it acts, it should stop before anything outward-facing and show exactly what will happen.
  • Does it stay out of the way? A companion should never cover your editor or steal focus.
  • Whose Claude does it use? Your own key or sign-in keeps billing and data in your account.
