AI agents for Mac: three kinds, and what to check first

November 27, 2026 · YOOANPAPA

Chat apps, screen-control agents and desktop companions all call themselves AI agents for Mac. Here is how they differ, and the one safety question to ask first.

"AI agent for Mac" now covers very different apps. Before you pick one, it helps to sort them by where they live and how they act.

Three kinds of AI agents for Mac

  1. Chat apps with an agent mode. You open a window, type a request, and the app answers. In agent mode it can browse or run steps for you. Great for questions and research, but it lives in a window you have to open, so it waits until you come to it.
  2. Screen-control (computer-use) agents. These look at your screen and move the mouse and keyboard for you, sometimes on a virtual machine or in the cloud. They can reach almost any app, but they work by driving the screen, which can be slow, and you usually step away while they do it.
  3. Desktop companions. A small character or widget stays on your desktop. Some only show the status of your coding agent; others take errands directly. They sit beside your work instead of taking over the screen.

The one question that matters: what does it do without asking?

Whatever kind you choose, look for a clear line between reading and acting outward.

  • Reading your calendar or summarizing a page is low risk.
  • Sending an email, posting, booking, paying or deleting cannot be undone.

A good agent shows you exactly what will go out (recipient, amount, attachment) and waits for you. It should also treat text inside emails and web pages as data, not instructions, so a hidden line in a web page can't tell it to send your files somewhere.

Other things to check

  • Whose AI does it use? Some agents bundle a subscription; others use your own API key, so usage is billed to you and your data goes to a provider you already trust.
  • Does it have its own server? If not, your requests go straight from your Mac to the AI provider.
  • Pricing. Subscription or one-time purchase.
  • Mac requirements. Apple silicon only or Intel too, and the minimum macOS version.
