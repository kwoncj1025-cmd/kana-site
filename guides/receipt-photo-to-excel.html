Receipt photo to Excel, without typing

November 28, 2026 · YOOANPAPA

Typing receipts into a spreadsheet is the chore nobody does. Here is how Kana turns a receipt photo into an Excel file with items, date, store and a checked total in under 30 seconds.

Expense reports pile up because typing each receipt line into a spreadsheet is dull. Photo-to-table apps exist, but you still have to export, fix columns and add a total.

With Kana

  1. Drag the receipt photo onto Kana (a screenshot or a photo from your phone both work).
  2. Say “Put this receipt in a spreadsheet.”
  3. Kana reads the items, prices, date and store, and saves an Excel file named for the month.
  4. The total is a real =SUM formula, and Kana checks it against the total printed on the receipt before saying she is done.

Real numbers

Measured on the November 28, 2026 release: a 4-item receipt became a spreadsheet in about 15–29 seconds, with no approval cards. In both runs the formula total matched the printed total.

Then what?

  • Say “Make it a PDF I can send” for a one-page PDF with fonts embedded.
  • Asking again (“do that again”) won't make a duplicate file; Kana tells you the table already exists.
  • Sending the file to your accountant is a send, so it stops at an approval card first.

Good to know

  • Blurry or crumpled receipts can be misread. Kana compares the total so you can spot it, but glance at the items.
  • Photos inside the Photos app can't be opened directly; drag the photo onto Kana instead.
