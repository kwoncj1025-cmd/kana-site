Where the others are ahead

  • They keep working while you're away. Muse gives every user “their own cloud computer” (Muse), and Duet works in the background “through its own virtual machine” (TechCrunch). One user's Duet greets him after his quiet hours with two things it finished overnight (Ryan Doser). Kana stops when your Mac sleeps.
  • They reach you away from your desk. You can chat with Muse on mobile, desktop or in WhatsApp (Muse). Kana is on your Mac desktop only.
  • They notice things on their own. In Zapier’s test, Dots flagged card payments that bounced and two deliveries that might clash, most of it unasked.

Where Kana is ahead

  • Speed on everyday chores. In a nine-task test, Duet “was also slow” (ChatPRD), and a separate review says its threads “could take 10-plus minutes” (ChatPRD Duet review). On Kana, simple questions answer in about 1.5–2.5 seconds, tidying 142 files in Downloads takes about 5 seconds with one card, and a hotel price check across Booking and Agoda takes about 30–40 seconds.
  • It asks per action, every time. A YouTuber says Muse accepted a lowball Marketplace offer and shared his address after he chose “Allow Always”; Meta says Muse followed his instructions and asked permission (Dexerto). Kana has no “allow always”: sending, paying, booking and deleting each stop at a card that shows exactly what will go out.
  • Your stuff stays on your Mac. There is no Kana server that keeps your chats or files. Besides Claude, the app goes online only for things like the license check (via Polar), update checks, and the sites or music you ask for. Her notebook (what to call you, your hours, your brand colors) is a file on your Mac that you can edit, and she never overwrites lines you changed. (For context: a journalist claimed Muse read his messages without permission, which Meta disputed; TechCrunch.)
  • Pay once. $19, not a monthly agent fee. You do need your own Claude plan or API key.
  • Someone is actually there. A hand-drawn character stands on your desktop and shows when she is working, waiting or done.

What Kana doesn’t do yet

  • No phone or messenger access yet. You talk to her on the Mac.
  • No cloud background work. Morning briefs and mail watching run only while your Mac is awake.
  • Apple silicon Macs with macOS 13 or later only.

Which should you pick?

If you want an agent that keeps working in the cloud and reaches you on your phone, Muse, Dots or Duet will suit you better. If you sit at a Mac most of the day and want small chores done fast, with a hard rule that nothing leaves your Mac without a press and hold, that is what Kana is built for.

FAQ

Is Kana a replacement for Muse, Dots or Duet?
Not fully. Kana runs only on your Mac while it is awake and has no phone or messenger access. It is fast on everyday Mac chores and asks before every outward action.
How fast is Kana?
Measured on a real Mac with the November 28, 2026 release: simple questions in about 1.5–2.5 seconds, tidying 142 files in Downloads in about 5 seconds with one approval card, and a Booking and Agoda hotel price check in about 30–40 seconds.
What does Kana cost?
Kana is a one-time $19 purchase (launch price). It thinks with Claude Code on your Mac, so you also need a Claude Pro or Max plan or an Anthropic API key.
Does Kana send anything without asking?
No. Sending, posting, booking, paying and deleting always stop at an approval card that you press and hold. There is no “allow always” option.
